Freetown, Sierra Leone

Freetown, located on the Sierra Leone Peninsula along the Atlantic coast, was established as a haven for freed and destitute African slaves, known as the Black Poor, who had been living in England. The city was selected by English abolitionist Granville Sharp in 1787, and later settled by African Americans from Nova Scotia, Jamaican Maroons, and liberated Africans from captured slave ships under the guidance of abolitionist John Clarkson in 1792
